As an AI Engineer at Cottingham & Butler, I worked on Canyon, a SIP back-to-back user agent (B2BUA) that records and transcribes calls, tracks outbound desk calls, and runs voice agents for caller flows an IVR would otherwise handle. A transfer can leave two representatives on one recording. I used the time the second representative joined to split the transcript at the handoff, so each person can review their own part of the conversation.
