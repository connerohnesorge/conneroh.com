Quarterly Report Drafter turns a carrier’s report into a Word draft for a benefits consultant. The prescription section combines two kinds of information: what the plan paid for a drug and how the consultant describes that drug to the employer.

I kept those sources separate. If the drug is missing from the approved reference list, the draft still shows its reported tier and paid amount, but leaves the drug class and treatment use blank. It flags the missing wording for the consultant instead of filling it in with a model’s guess.
