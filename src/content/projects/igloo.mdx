Igloo gives teams a place to describe proposed work and compare its priority with other requests. I wrote the import that carries ratings from a portfolio board into Igloo.

The two systems ask different questions. The board rated business value and customer impact separately; Igloo uses one value rating. The import takes the higher of the two and keeps both originals so a reviewer can see where the new rating came from. It won’t overwrite an existing rating on the matched project.
