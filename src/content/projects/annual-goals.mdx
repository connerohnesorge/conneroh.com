groups: [cnb]

---

Practice leaders enter producer goals in Annual Goals, and finance staff record department and practice targets. Once a goal is approved, changing it means opening a revision rather than editing the number used for reporting.

I added a check that compares a revision’s effective date with the year of the goal. A December date on next year’s target, for example, is rejected even though it is a perfectly valid calendar date.
