Dossier starts with what an insurance producer already knows about a prospect. It brings company research, potential risks and questions for the meeting into one brief, with citations the producer can follow.

I added a way to request and read a brief through an AI assistant. The assistant starts the same research job as the web form and gets a job ID back rather than keeping the conversation waiting. Once the brief is ready, the assistant can read the overview and request a single section. A producer asking who might be at the table can consult the stakeholder map without pulling the entire report into the conversation.
