Benefits consultants use Annual Notices to assemble the notices that apply to an employer’s health plan. The consultant answers the plan questions once; those answers determine which notices go into the Word packet.

I added the choice of English, Spanish, or both. Each language has its own document template, so requesting both uses the same plan answers without asking the consultant to translate the finished notices.
