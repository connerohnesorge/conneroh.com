Captive insurance teams collect quarterly fuel-tax filings from member carriers. Captive IFTA organizes the miles by jurisdiction, member, and filing period. I built the import for mileage already recorded in older workbooks.

Before writing anything, the importer shows which filings are new, which match, and which would change an existing record. The reviewer must acknowledge each overwrite separately: accepting a workbook is not blanket permission to replace its entire history.
