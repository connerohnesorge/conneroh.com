Casebook lets researchers choose a report configuration, follow the search as it runs, and return to the finished report. My contribution was the identity check on requests to the app.

Rather than trust an email address simply because it appears on a request, I connected Casebook to a verifier that checks the gateway’s signature. The app uses the verified identity to identify the caller and rejects the request if verification fails.
